Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Amazon AI Operations OS is an internal business application used to access authorized Amazon Advertising account data for advertising reporting, analytics, optimization, and authorized campaign management.

1. Information We Access

When authorized by the account owner, the application may access Amazon Advertising account information and advertising performance data, including:

  • Advertising profiles and account identifiers
  • Campaigns, ad groups, keywords, targets, and campaign settings
  • Search terms, placements, and advertised product information
  • Advertising performance metrics such as impressions, clicks, spend, orders, and sales
  • Other advertising data made available through authorized Amazon Ads APIs

2. How We Use Information

Authorized data is used only for internal business purposes, including advertising reporting, analytics, performance monitoring, optimization, investigation, and authorized campaign management.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent Amazon Advertising data or personal information. Data is not shared with unrelated third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Credentials and Security

Access credentials and authorization tokens are intended to be stored securely and used only to access accounts for which authorization has been granted. Access to advertising data is limited to authorized business use.

5. Data Retention

Advertising data may be retained for operational reporting, historical analysis, auditing, and performance comparison for as long as reasonably required for legitimate internal business purposes or applicable legal obligations.

6. Revoking Access

Authorization may be revoked through the relevant Amazon account or application authorization settings. After access is revoked, the application will no longer be able to retrieve new data using the revoked authorization.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, data use, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
